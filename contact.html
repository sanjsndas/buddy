📬 Get in touch

At Buddy, we're passionate about pets and dedicated to providing the best care resources for you and your furry, feathered, or scaly friends. Whether you have a question about our content, need advice on pet care, want to share your story, or have feedback for us, we're here to listen. Your input helps us grow and improve, and we value every message we receive.

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent pet health concerns, please contact your veterinarian immediately — we are not a medical service provider, but we're always happy to point you in the right direction.

We're pet lovers just like you. Every message is read by a real person who cares about your pet's well-being.

📋 Frequently asked questions

Before reaching out, you might find the answer to your question here:

  • How quickly do you respond? We typically respond within 24-48 hours during business days.
  • Can I submit a guest post or story? Absolutely! We love featuring community stories. Please use the form above and select "Content Question" as the subject.
  • Do you offer veterinary advice? No, we are not veterinarians. We provide educational content only. For medical concerns, please consult a licensed veterinarian.
  • How can I partner with Buddy? We're always open to collaborations. Select "Partnership / Collaboration" in the subject dropdown.
  • Is there a physical location I can visit? Yes, our office is located at 42 Pawsome Lane, Austin, TX. Please call ahead to ensure someone is available to meet you.
