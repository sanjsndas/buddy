Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to enhance your experience

🍪 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide information to the website owners. Cookies help us remember your preferences, analyze how you interact with our site, and deliver personalized content.

At Buddy, we use cookies to improve your browsing experience, understand how you use our website, and tailor our content to your interests. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through the settings in your browser or by using the cookie consent dialog at the bottom of this page.

📋 Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

🔹 Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies as they are essential for the operation of the site.

  • Session cookies: These allow you to navigate the site and use its features, such as logging into your account.
  • Security cookies: These help us detect and prevent fraudulent activity and protect your data.
  • Load balancing cookies: These distribute traffic across servers to ensure optimal performance.

🔹 Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our site, fix issues, and create better content for our users.

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to track page views, user behavior, and traffic sources. This helps us understand which content is most popular and how users navigate our site.
  • Heat mapping tools: We may use tools to understand where users click and scroll, helping us improve page layout and design.

🔹 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language, region, or font size) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may also be used to remember changes you've made to text size, fonts, and other parts of web pages that you can customize.

  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for future visits.
  • User interface customization: Remember your preferred layout or display options.

🔹 Targeting & Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver content that is more relevant to you and your interests. They may be used to deliver targeted advertising or to limit the number of times you see an advertisement. They also help us measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

  • Social media cookies: Allow you to share content on social media platforms.
  • Retargeting cookies: Show you relevant ads on other websites based on your browsing behavior.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for advertising purposes without your explicit consent. Any third-party cookies are subject to the privacy policies of those third parties.

🛠️ How to manage your cookie preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  1. Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to block all cookies, accept only certain cookies, or alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
  2. Cookie consent dialog: At the bottom of this page, you will find a cookie consent dialog that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your choice will be stored for future visits.
  3. Third-party opt-out tools: You can opt out of third-party analytics and advertising cookies using tools such as the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page or the Digital Advertising Alliance's Consumer Choice page.

For more information about managing cookies in your browser, please visit the help section of your browser or refer to the following links:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

📈 How we use cookie data

We use the data collected through cookies to:

  • Improve user experience: By remembering your preferences and settings, we can provide a more personalized experience.
  • Analyze website performance: We track how users interact with our site to identify areas for improvement and optimize content.
  • Enhance security: Cookies help us detect and prevent fraudulent activity, ensuring the safety of your data.
  • Tailor content: We use analytics data to understand which topics and resources are most valuable to our users, allowing us to create better content.
  • Measure marketing effectiveness: We track the performance of our marketing campaigns to ensure we are reaching the right audience effectively.

All data collected through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required for the operation of our site or as otherwise disclosed in our Privacy Policy.

🔗 Third-party cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies on our website. These services include:

  • Google Analytics: Provides analytics data to help us understand user behavior.
  • Social media platforms: Allow you to share content and connect with us on social media.
  • Advertising partners: Help us deliver relevant advertisements to you across the web.

We do not control the cookies set by third parties, and we recommend that you review the privacy policies of these third parties for more information about their cookie practices.

⏳ Cookie retention

The retention period for cookies varies depending on their purpose:

  • Session cookies: These are temporary and expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a set period (ranging from days to years) or until you delete them manually.

We use persistent cookies to remember your preferences for future visits, which enhances your experience by reducing the need to re-enter settings. You can delete persistent cookies through your browser settings at any time.
